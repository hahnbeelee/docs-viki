## What Can You Build?

Yelp APIs are split into two main categories:

- **Yelp Places API** — Publicly accessible via self-serve. Search businesses, read reviews, browse events, and query rich business data. Available to all developers.
- **Yelp Partner APIs** — Advanced APIs for advertising, leads, reporting, listing management, and more. Requires a Yelp Partner agreement.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Yelp App">
    Visit [business.yelp.com/data/products/places-api](https://business.yelp.com/data/products/places-api/) to choose a plan and create your app. You'll receive an API key automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Your Requests">
    Pass your API key in the `Authorization` header as `Bearer YOUR_API_KEY` on every request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make Your First Call">
    Search for businesses near a location using the Business Search endpoint at `https://api.yelp.com/v3/businesses/search`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Docs">
    Browse the guides for the Places API, AI API, or Partner APIs depending on your use case.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Partner APIs require a separate agreement with Yelp. If you're unsure which APIs you have access to, contact your Yelp business development or partnership team.
</Note>

# Source: https://docs.developer.yelp.com/docs/yelp-platform

Yelp Platform allows 3rd party developers to integrate their transaction services for local businesses directly on Yelp sites and apps. Integration is easy, with 3 simple steps:

### 

[Data Ingestion API](https://docs.developer.yelp.com/docs/data-ingestion-api)

Use the Data Ingestion API to match your businesses with businesses on Yelp.

### 

[Transaction Iframe](https://docs.developer.yelp.com/docs/iframe-interactions)

Expose your transaction services through an iframe for desktop and mobile.

### 

[Yelp Checkout API](https://docs.developer.yelp.com/docs/checkout-api)

Integrate your ordering, scheduling, payment, fulfillment, and customer support processes into the transaction flow.

### 

How can I get started?

We have rolled out Yelp Platform with food delivery, spas, salons and dentist appointments. If you are interested in partnering with us, we’d love to hear from you! You can contact us at [bizdev@yelp.com](mailto:bizdev@yelp.com) and browse the Platform APIs documentation.

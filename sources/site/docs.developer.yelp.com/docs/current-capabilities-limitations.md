# Source: https://docs.developer.yelp.com/docs/current-capabilities-limitations

This page outlines what is currently supported by the Yelp AI API, and functionalities that are not currently supported.

## 

Overview

**What is supported today?**

- Business searches with contextual search summaries (e.g. “Popular Mexican restaurants in Orange County, LA that are reasonably priced”)
- Business questions & answers (e.g. “What are the popular dishes at the above restaurants?”)
- Business one-shot questions & answers (e.g. "Does Capital Grille in Costa Mesa, CA offer vegan options?")
- Business comparisons (e.g., “Compare Red King and Original King ramen dishes at Ramen Nagi in Palo Alto”)
- Making restaurant reservations (e.g., "I’d like a reservation for four at Chubby Cattle in Chicago this Friday at 8pm")

**What is not yet supported?**

- Updating or canceling restaurant reservations and signing up for waitlists
- Requesting quotes from Services professionals
- Providing turn-by-turn directions
- Other advanced or specialized tasks outside the business domain

 

## 

Business search with contextual search summaries

The Yelp AI API can search and recommend businesses based on user context such as location, category, and preferences.

### 

Example Request

JSON

`POST https://api.yelp.com/ai/chat/v2 Authorization: Bearer <Your API Key> Content-Type: application/json  {   "query": "Popular Mexican restaurants in Orange County, LA that are reasonably priced",   "user_context": {     "locale": "en_US",     "latitude": 33.7175,     "longitude": -117.8311   } }`

### 

Relevant Fields

- **response.text**: General textual answer to the user’s query.
- **response.tags\[\]**: Tags that mark specific portions of the response text, including business names and highlights.
 - **tag\_type**: Identifies whether the tag marks a "business" or "highlight" in the response.
 - **start/end**: Character positions in the response text for the tagged content.
 - **meta.business\_id**: Links tagged business names to their corresponding business data.
- **types**: Indicates the type of response (e.g., "business\_search").
- **entities.businesses\[\]**: Array of businesses matching the query. Within each business, the following fields are particularly relevant.
 - **id**: Unique identifier for the business, useful for referencing details or linking to more info.
 - **name**: The restaurant’s name, directly answering the user’s request for specific Mexican restaurants.
 - **url**: A link to the business’s full listing, providing further information.
 - **location**: Address and city (e.g., Orange, Costa Mesa), confirming the business is in Orange County.
 - **coordinates**: Latitude and longitude of the restaurant, confirming proximity to the requested area.
 - **review\_count**: Number of reviews, indicating popularity.
 - **price**: Signifies how expensive the restaurant is (“$” or “$$”), helpful for determining if it is “reasonably priced.”
 - **rating**: User rating (e.g., 4.6), addressing “popular” or well-reviewed businesses.
 - **categories**: Includes "mexican" in the category alias or title, confirming the cuisine type.
 - **summaries / contextual\_info\_summary**: Provides a concise overview of the restaurant and whether it meets the user’s needs (e.g., authenticity, price, specialties).
- **chat\_id**: The identifier for the user’s chat session

 - **start/end**: Character positions in the response text for the tagged content.
 - **meta.business\_id**: Links tagged business names to their corresponding business data.
- **types**: Indicates the type of response (e.g., "business\_question").
- **entities.businesses\[\]**: Array of businesses being discussed. For each business, fields of interest for popular dishes:
 - **id**: Unique identifier for each restaurant, useful for linking dishes to specific establishments.
 - **name**: The name of the restaurant associated with the popular dishes.
 - **summaries.short/medium/long**: May include mentions of signature dishes or specialties (e.g., "focus on mole samplers and tlayudas", "Martin's platter, taco sampler").
 - **attributes.AboutThisBizSpecialties**: Business-provided information about their specialty dishes and offerings.
 - **attributes.MenuUrl**: Direct link to the restaurant's menu for additional dish information.
 - **contextual\_info.review\_snippets\[\]**: Customer reviews that mention specific popular dishes and recommendations:
 - **comment**: Review text that may contain dish names and customer recommendations (e.g., "must try the mole negro", "highly recommend the taco sampler").
 - **rating**: Rating associated with the review, indicating customer satisfaction with mentioned dishes.

### 

Relevant Fields

- **response.text**: General textual answer to the user's query about restaurant availability and reservation options.
- **response.tags\[\]**: Array of tags marking important text elements. Within each tag:
 - **tag\_type**: Type of tag (e.g., "business" for restaurant names, "highlight" for emphasized text).
 - **start/end**: Character positions for the tagged text in the response.
 - **meta.business\_id**: Links the tagged business name to the full business entity.
- **types**: Indicates the type of response (e.g., "reservation").
- **entities.businesses\[\]**: Array of businesses matching the query. Within each business, the following fields are particularly relevant:
 - **id**: Unique identifier for the business, useful for referencing details or linking to more info.
 - **name**: The restaurant's name (e.g., "Tuscany Steakhouse").
 - **url**: A link to the business's full Yelp listing, providing further information.
 - **location**: Full address information including street, city, state, and formatted address.
 - **coordinates**: Latitude and longitude of the restaurant for mapping purposes.
 - **review\_count**: Number of reviews (281), indicating popularity and reliability of ratings.
 - **price**: Price range indicator ("$"), showing upscale pricing.
 - **rating**: User rating (4.6), indicating overall customer satisfaction.
 - **categories**: Restaurant types (e.g., "Steakhouses", "Italian", "Seafood").
 - **phone**: Contact number for direct reservations or inquiries.
 - **attributes**: Detailed business attributes including:
 - **RestaurantsReservations**: Confirms if reservations are accepted (true).
 - **Ambience**: Atmosphere descriptors (e.g., "classy", "upscale").
 - **GoodForMeal**: Indicates which meals are served (dinner, dessert).
 - **summaries**: Multiple length descriptions of the restaurant highlighting key features.
 - **contextual\_info.photos**: Array of photo URLs showing the restaurant's appearance.

 

## 

Limitations

Currently, the Yelp AI API does not support:

- Providing step-by-step directions to a business location.
- Non-business-related queries (e.g., health advice, financial advice).

We’re continually improving and expanding the feature set. Keep an eye on our [Changelog](https://docs.developer.yelp.com/changelog) for updates on upcoming capabilities!
